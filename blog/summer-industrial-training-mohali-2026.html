⏱️ 6-Week vs 6-Month — Which Is Right for You?

The most common question students ask: "Should I do the 6-week summer training or the full 6-month training?" The honest answer depends on your year of study, your goals, and your university's requirements. Here's the complete breakdown:

Most Popular

6-Week Training

Perfect for summer break — May to July

Fits perfectly inside summer break without academic conflict
1 complete live project + certificate
Core skills: frontend, backend, or AI basics
Lower cost — great ROI for 2nd & 3rd year students
Limited depth — won't cover advanced topics
Less portfolio-worthy than longer programs
✅ Best For:

2nd & 3rd year B.Tech/BCA students who want summer training certification and foundational project experience.

Recommended for Final Year

6-Month Training

Complete transformation — December to June or July to December

Industry-equivalent experience on your resume
3–5 live projects across full stack or specialization
Interview preparation + soft skills + mock placements
Advanced topics: DevOps, AI, cloud, system design
Strong LinkedIn profile and GitHub portfolio
Longer commitment — needs scheduling with college
✅ Best For:

Final year & pre-final year students who want placement-ready skills and a serious edge in campus interviews.

Not sure which to pick? Most universities require a minimum 6-week summer training report for degree completion. Final year students are strongly encouraged to choose 3 or 6 months for placement advantage. Talk to our advisor — we'll guide you based on your university.
