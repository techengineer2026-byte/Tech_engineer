The Challenge

Real-time gaming requires absolute precision and massive concurrency.

The Aviator project needed a robust backend capable of handling thousands of simultaneous connections without lag. Additionally, providing a secure and lightweight APK distribution method was critical for user growth outside traditional app stores.

The Solution

A microservices architecture built for speed.

  • WebSocket integration for instant data updates.
  • Flutter-based cross-platform performance.
  • Secure, encrypted APK distribution system.
