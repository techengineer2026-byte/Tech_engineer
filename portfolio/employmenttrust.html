The Big Idea

Job Hunting Meets Swipe Culture

Traditional job boards are overwhelming and outdated. We reimagined the experience with a Tinder-inspired card interface where job seekers swipe right on opportunities they love and employers swipe on candidates who fit perfectly.

Three User Types, One Ecosystem

Job Seekers

Swipe through curated job cards, save favorites, and get instant matches with employers

Employers

Post positions, review candidate profiles, and connect with talent through smart matching

ET Trust Center

Verified credentials, background checks, and quality assurance for safe hiring

CORE FEATURE

Swipe to Match

Intuitive card-based job browsing

Job seekers see beautifully designed cards with key details — company logo, position, salary, location. Swipe right to apply, left to skip. No endless scrolling through irrelevant listings.

  • Instant visual job preview
  • One-tap application process
  • Smart algorithm learns preferences
EMPLOYER PORTAL

Recruiter Dashboard

Manage candidates like a pro

Employers get a clean dashboard to post jobs, review applicant cards, track matches, and manage the entire hiring pipeline from one interface.

  • Applicant tracking system
  • Analytics & hiring metrics
  • Team collaboration tools
VERIFICATION

ET Trust Center

Safety and authenticity first

Built-in verification system ensures both job seekers and employers are legitimate. Background checks, credential validation, and company verification protect all users.

  • ID verification badges
  • Background screening integration
  • Fraud prevention system
