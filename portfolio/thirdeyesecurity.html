The Challenge

27 years of experience hidden behind an invisible online presence.

Despite being a veteran-led, licensed security company serving Kamloops for nearly three decades, Third Eye Security had no digital footprint. Potential clients couldn't verify credentials, see patrol fleet, or request quotes online — resulting in lost contracts to competitors with stronger web presence.

The Solution

A command-center inspired website that radiates authority and trust.

  • Live System Status Dashboard Header
  • Credential & License Showcase
  • Service Portfolio with Tactical Icons
  • Fleet Gallery for Operational Transparency
  • Community Impact Section (Hospice Drive)

Design Strategy

Authority First

Dark tactical theme with red accents conveys professional security command

Credential Display

Prominent licensing, training, and experience badges build instant trust

Urgency CTAs

24/7 dispatch hotline visible at all times for emergency response

Standout Features

Live System Status

Animated "SYSTEM: ONLINE" ticker in header creates immediate operational credibility and mirrors dispatch command centers.

Fleet Gallery

Real patrol vehicle photos show branded units on active duty — proving legitimacy and local presence in Kamloops.

Community Impact

$4,000 Hospice donation story humanizes the brand and demonstrates local commitment beyond profit.

Career Portal

Built-in recruitment section attracts licensed BC security professionals — supporting business growth.
