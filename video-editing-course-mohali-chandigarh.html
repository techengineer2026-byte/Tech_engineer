Video Editing Course FAQs - Answers to Your Questions

Is this course for beginners?

Yes! Our course is designed for everyone, from complete beginners to those looking to upgrade their skills. We start with the fundamentals and build up to advanced techniques.

What is the course duration?

The complete course is 3 months long. We offer flexible batch timings, including weekday and weekend options to fit your schedule.

Will I get a certificate?

Absolutely. Upon successful completion, you will receive an industry-recognized certificate from TechEngineer to showcase on your resume and portfolio.

Will this course help me build a career in Video Editing?

Yes, absolutely. Our course is meticulously designed to transform you from a beginner into a job-ready professional. We focus on practical, hands-on skills and building a strong portfolio that will impress employers.

Can I get a job after completion?

While we cannot guarantee a job, we provide 100% job assistance. This includes resume building, interview preparation, and connecting you with our network of hiring partners. Our goal is to make you as employable as possible.

Will this course help me grow on social media?

Definitely. We dedicate modules to editing for social media, covering techniques for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and other platforms. You'll learn how to create fast-paced, engaging content that captures attention and helps grow an online presence.

Do you cover AI tools in the course?

Yes, we embrace modern technology. The course covers the latest AI-powered features in tools like Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, plus we introduce you to standalone AI tools that can speed up your workflow for tasks like transcription and color grading.

Is the course online or offline?

We offer both. You can join our offline, in-person classes at our institute in Mohali for a traditional classroom experience, or you can opt for our live, interactive online classes if you prefer to learn from home.

Are the classes recorded or live?

Our classes are 100% live and interactive, both online and offline. We believe in direct interaction with expert instructors, allowing you to ask questions and get instant feedback. We do not use pre-recorded videos for our main training.

Will you provide training on editing a live podcast?

Yes! This is one of our unique features. We provide hands-on training on editing multi-camera podcast episodes in our own Puadh Punjabi Podcast studio (PPP). This is a highly valuable and in-demand skill in the current market.
