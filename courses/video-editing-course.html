Course Overview

What You'll Learn in Our Video Editing Course

This isn't a theoretical class; it's a hands-on workshop designed to make you a skilled video editor. We focus on the two most powerful tools in the industry: Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects. You'll move from understanding the basics of a timeline to creating compelling stories, stunning visual effects, and professional motion graphics.

Our training in Mohali is structured to simulate a real production environment. You'll learn about color grading, sound design, multi-cam editing, and green screen keying. Every module is project-based, ensuring you are constantly building your skills and your professional portfolio.

Key Software & Skills

  • Adobe Premiere Pro: Professional editing, color correction, audio mixing, and final exports.
  • Adobe After Effects: Motion graphics, VFX, compositing, and dynamic text animations.
  • Storytelling & Pacing: Learn the art of crafting a narrative that engages viewers from start to finish.
