FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our video editing services.

How much does video editing cost?

Our pricing starts at ₹500 for Instagram Reels (30-60 sec) and ₹2,500 for YouTube videos (10-15 min). For longer videos or advanced editing with motion graphics, we offer custom quotes. Bulk packages are also available at discounted rates.

How long does it take to edit a video?

Instagram Reels are delivered within 24 hours. YouTube videos (10-15 min) take 2-3 days. Longer or more complex videos with motion graphics may take 5-7 days. We also offer rush delivery for an additional fee.

Do you provide unlimited revisions?

Yes! All our packages include unlimited revisions until you're 100% satisfied with the final video. Your happiness is our priority.

What file formats do you accept and deliver?

We accept all major video formats (MP4, MOV, AVI, etc.). We deliver final videos in MP4 (most common), MOV, or any format you require, optimized for your platform (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, etc.) in HD, Full HD, or 4K.

Can you add subtitles and captions?

Absolutely! We can add hardcoded subtitles (burned into the video) or provide separate SRT files. Subtitles are great for social media engagement and accessibility.

Do you provide stock footage and music?

Yes, we can source royalty-free stock footage, images, and background music from premium libraries like Artlist, Epidemic Sound, and Storyblocks (additional charges may apply).
