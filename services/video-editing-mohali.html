Our Video Editing Process

From raw footage to a polished final cut, we follow a simple, collaborative process to bring your vision to life.

Start Your Project
01

Upload & Briefing

You securely upload your raw footage and provide us with your project brief, style references, and creative vision.

02

First Draft & Review

Our editors create the first cut of your video, including music, basic color grading, and graphics, and share it for your feedback.

03

Delivery

You receive the final, high-resolution video file in your desired format, ready to be published on any platform.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our video editing services.

How long does it take to edit a video?

Turnaround time depends on the complexity. Simple Instagram Reels are typically delivered within 24-48 hours. Longer YouTube videos or complex edits can take 3-5 business days.

What do I need to provide to get started?

You just need to provide us with the raw video footage (via Google Drive, WeTransfer, etc.), any specific logos or brand assets, and a brief outline of your vision or a reference video you like.

How many revisions are included?

We offer up to two rounds of revisions for our per-project plans to ensure you are 100% satisfied. Our monthly retainer plans include unlimited revisions for ultimate flexibility.
