FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI chatbot development in Mohali.

How much does an AI chatbot cost?

AI chatbot cost in Mohali: Starts at ₹25,000 for a basic website bot and can go up to ₹1,50,000+ for complex WhatsApp bots with custom integrations.

Can you integrate the chatbot with WhatsApp?

Yes, absolutely. We specialize in developing WhatsApp chatbots using the official WhatsApp Business API. This allows you to automate conversations, send notifications, and provide support directly on WhatsApp.

What can an AI chatbot do for my business?

An AI chatbot can automate customer support by answering common questions 24/7, capture and qualify leads from your website, book appointments, take orders, and provide personalized user experiences, saving you time and increasing revenue.

What makes your chatbots 'AI'?

Our AI chatbots are powered by Large Language Models (LLMs) like OpenAI's GPT. We use advanced frameworks like LangChain and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) to train the bot on your specific business data. This allows it to understand complex user intent and provide natural, human-like answers instead of just following a script.

Do you offer maintenance after the chatbot is live?

Yes. All our packages come with a free support period. After that, we offer affordable monthly maintenance plans that include performance monitoring, conversation analysis, and AI model retraining to ensure your chatbot continues to get smarter and more effective over time.
