FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about WhatsApp marketing.

Is sending bulk WhatsApp messages legal?

Yes, when done through the official WhatsApp Business API, it is 100% legal and compliant. We help you get verified by Meta and use pre-approved message templates for campaigns to users who have opted-in. We do not use illegal methods that can get your number blocked.

What is the WhatsApp Business API?

The WhatsApp Business API is an official platform from Meta (Facebook) designed for businesses. It allows you to send messages at scale, build chatbots for automation, and integrate WhatsApp with your CRM and other business software.

How much does it cost?

There are two costs: our service fee for setup and management, and Meta's per-conversation charges. Our campaign setup starts at ₹10,000. We provide a full, transparent cost breakdown in our free consultation.
