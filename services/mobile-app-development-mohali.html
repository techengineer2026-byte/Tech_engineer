FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mobile app development in Mohali.

How much does a mobile app cost in Mohali?

A basic app for a single platform (iOS or Android) starts at ₹50,000. A more professional app for both platforms can cost ₹1,20,000 or more, depending on the complexity, features, and design.

How long does it take to build an app?

A simple app can be built in 30-45 days. A more complex app with a custom backend and APIs can take 60-90 days or longer. We provide a detailed project timeline after the initial discovery phase.

Do you build for both iOS and Android?

Yes. We offer native development for both iOS (using Swift) and Android (using Kotlin). We also build cross-platform apps with React Native or Flutter, which allows you to have one codebase for both platforms, saving time and cost.

Can you help with App Store Optimization (ASO)?

Yes. ASO is the 'SEO for apps'. We help with keyword research, writing compelling app descriptions, creating high-quality screenshots, and setting up your App Store & Play Store listings to maximize visibility and downloads.
