Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI voice agent development in India.

How much does an AI voice agent cost?

A basic AI voice agent for simple tasks like appointment reminders might start at ₹40,000. More advanced agents that handle complex conversations and integrate with CRMs can range from ₹90,000 to ₹2,00,000+.

Can the voice agent sound human-like?

Yes, absolutely. We use state-of-the-art text-to-speech (TTS) and speech-to-text (STT) engines from providers like Google and ElevenLabs to create natural, human-like conversations. We can customize the voice, tone, and language.

What languages do you support?

Our AI voice agents can be developed to support multiple languages, including English, Hindi, Punjabi, and other regional Indian languages, to cater to a diverse customer base.
