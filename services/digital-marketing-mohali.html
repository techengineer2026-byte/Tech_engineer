FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about digital marketing in Mohali.

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

SEO is a long-term strategy. While some improvements can be seen in the first 1-2 months, it typically takes 3-6 months to see significant, stable results in search rankings and organic traffic.

Is SEO or Google Ads better for my business?

It depends on your goals. Google Ads provides immediate traffic and leads, but you pay for every click. SEO provides long-term, 'free' organic traffic but takes time to build. Often, the best strategy is a combination of both.

Do you guarantee #1 rankings on Google?

No reputable agency can guarantee a specific ranking on Google, as the algorithm is constantly changing. However, we guarantee that we will use the latest, industry-best practices and data-driven strategies to significantly improve your online visibility and search performance.

What's included in your digital marketing services?

Our comprehensive services include a full audit, strategy development, keyword research, on-page and off-page SEO, Google Ads management, content creation, and detailed monthly performance reporting.

Which businesses do you work with in Mohali?

We work with a wide range of local businesses in Mohali and the Tricity area, including educational institutes, restaurants, clinics, real estate agents, e-commerce stores, and B2B service providers.
